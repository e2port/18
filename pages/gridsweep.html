Rules & pattern hints Every drive starts at the 30 and runs through 8 puzzles, 18 tiles wide. The deeper you get on the field, the more rows each puzzle has (1 up to 6).
Offense: the wall is at the top, so numbers only sit below the puzzle. No flags. Clear every safe tile. Each mine you open costs a down (4 footballs per drive). Clear all 8 for a touchdown (+7). Lose all 4 and it’s a turnover on downs.
Defense: the wall is at the bottom, so numbers sit above the puzzle. Flags and clearing both work, and you finish a puzzle by clearing every safe tile or flagging every mine. Each mine you open lets the opponent break through 17.5 yards, so 4 mistakes is an opponent touchdown. Clear all 8 for a defensive stand.
One offensive and one defensive drive per quarter.

1-2-1: mines under the 1s, safe under the 2. 1-2-2-1: mines under the 2s, safe under the 1s. 1-2-X: the X is a mine. 1-1-X from a border: the X is safe. Subtract known mines from numbers, then look for the pattern again.
